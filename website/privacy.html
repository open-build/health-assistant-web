close to home.

This is an overview of the current app’s privacy architecture, based on the source reviewed on October 31, 2026. It is not a claim of certification or a substitute for a release-specific privacy policy.

Records live on your device.

Health Assistant stores your records locally. Note bodies, extracted text, provider settings, and original documents use record-bound AES-256-GCM encryption. Keys are held in the device Keychain. Timestamps and other sorting metadata use file protection rather than field-level encryption. The app includes app lock, export, and deletion controls.

AI is optional. Location matters.

  • Apple on-device: inference runs on a supported Apple device, subject to model availability.
  • Ollama or LM Studio: approved context travels to a computer on your local network. Local network HTTP traffic is not encrypted; the app requires acknowledgment.
  • A remote endpoint: approved context leaves your device for the configured HTTPS provider. Its operator, terms, and retention practices matter, including when you bring your own token.

The app provides a “Review What’s Shared” flow before networked sends. There is no automatic cloud AI fallback. Open-weight models have their own licenses and limitations; they are not automatically medically reliable.

Sharing is an explicit action.

You can export a visit brief or other PDF report, export records, and choose where to send them. Copies you share are then handled by the destination you choose. Recording a conversation requires consent from everyone involved.

This website.

This marketing site does not include analytics, advertising scripts, cookies, account registration, or a health-data submission form. The hosting provider may process standard request information when you load a page. External links take you to services with their own privacy practices.
