A home on your device.

Encrypted records and originals, with app lock and no required account or cloud sync.

CLOSER TO HOME

Helpful context.
Under your control.

Run your choice of open-source or open-weight model through Ollama or LM Studio on your local network. Apple’s on-device model is a separate option on supported devices.

Open-source and open-weight are not interchangeable: available code, weights, and usage rights depend on each model’s license. Local network models run on your computer; setup is required.

A companion for understanding and preparation. Health Assistant does not diagnose, recommend treatment changes, or replace your clinician.

THE OPEN-BUILD ECOSYSTEM

Meet BabbleBeaver.
More choice, by design.

Our open-build BabbleBeaver project provides an open-source AI integration layer for connecting different models and providers. Its modular approach supports local Ollama integrations alongside hosted services, making model choice part of the architecture.

Explore BabbleBeaver on GitHub ↗
INTEGRATION DIRECTION

Open app → flexible integration → your model

BabbleBeaver is the foundation for the broader integration direction: reusable provider connections, locally hosted models, and optional paid services using your own credentials.

The current iOS build connects directly to Apple’s on-device model, Ollama, LM Studio, and compatible endpoints. A BabbleBeaver connection has not yet been verified in this build. Hosting location, data handling, and model licenses remain choices to review for each deployment.

LOOKING AHEAD · RESEARCH & ANALYSIS

From health claims
to clearer evidence.

Planned analytics to help you explore what has
been studied, what guidelines support, and what remains uncertain.

01 / NUTRITION

Look beyond the food claim.

Build toward comparing nutrition approaches with published research and established dietary guidance, with source links and an explanation of the strength and limits of the evidence.

02 / EXERCISE

Understand the research behind movement.

Explore scientifically studied exercise methods, the populations they were tested in, and the outcomes measured—so a promising result is not mistaken for a universal recommendation.

03 / GENERAL HEALTH

Bring better context to care.

Develop analysis that helps distinguish a personal trend, a research finding, and a guideline-backed approach. Bring useful questions and traceable sources to conversations with your clinician.

A foundation today. More validation ahead.

The current Food & Facts code includes USDA nutrition lookups and comparisons with study abstracts from Europe PMC. Live-service testing and clinical review remain on the development checklist. Broader nutrition, exercise, and health analytics are roadmap work, not a clinically validated recommendation service.

Published research does not automatically mean a method is approved or appropriate for everyone. Future analysis should name the source, distinguish evidence quality from formal approval, and make uncertainty visible. “Analytics” here means health and evidence analysis, not advertising or visitor tracking.

A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

A clearer starting point.

Can I download it from the App Store?

We’re not linking an App Store release yet. Explore the app source for build instructions and follow the Buildly Forge project for distribution updates.

Do I need an AI subscription?

No. Appointments, notes, documents, and visit preparation work without AI. If you enable AI, you choose the provider. Local models require a compatible setup; paid services may charge separately.

Does “local-first” mean nothing ever leaves my phone?

Your records are stored locally. If you choose a networked AI provider, approved context is sent to that provider. You can also explicitly export or share reports. See the privacy model for the distinctions.

Can I share information with someone helping me?

You can export reports and choose what to share yourself. Live caregiver accounts, shared workspaces, and cloud sync are not current app features.
